🎯 Think about it: how does being isolated affect mental health?

Living in a small ship, far from family and friends, can make anyone feel lonely, stressed or sad. This is called isolation and confinement. It can disturb sleep, mood and teamwork, just like a tired body. That's why NASA studies the mind as much as the heart. Talking, sleeping well and keeping routines is real medicine!

    🚴 SpaceFit · CEVIS bike +5 XP

    👆 Tap the astronaut to start pedaling, then keep tapping to pedal faster!
    0strokes
    Ready?

    Without gravity, bones and muscles "fall asleep". That's why astronauts pedal every day (SpaceFit experiment).

    🚴 Meet the CEVIS bike (Cycle Ergometer with Vibration Isolation and Stabilization), the exercise bike on the space station. It has no wheels: it is bolted to the floor and the astronaut is held in by straps, so pedaling does not make them float away.

    Alternate the pedals as fast as you can for 10 seconds! The easiest way: tap the astronaut on the bike. Each tap is one pedal stroke.

    Keyboard: A and L, or ← and →. On phones: tap the buttons alternately.
