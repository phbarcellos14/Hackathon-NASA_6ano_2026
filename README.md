# 🚀 ASLS — Space Life Saving

> **One Hub. One Game. One App. One Mission.**

ASLS brings together, in one place, everything a crew member needs to look after the team’s health: **before the mission, to train**, and **during the mission, to act**.

## 🌟 What’s Inside the Hub

### 🎮 PLAY — 3D Training
Inside a 3D spacecraft cabin, the player takes the role of the crew medical officer:
- Measure blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen saturation, and temperature.
- Listen to the heart and lungs with a guided 3D stethoscope.
- Run triage, shelter from solar radiation storms, and practice a cardiac-arrest response in microgravity using an **AED, CPR, and the LUCAS 3 device**.
- Every exam earns **XP** and teaches why each check matters in space.

### 🩺 SPACE DOC — Decision-Support App
A live health dashboard with **13 screens** that turns every reading into a **green, yellow, or red alert**:
- **Autonomous layer:** releases urgent actions that cannot wait.
- **Assisted layer:** guides every dose step by step.
- **Handoff layer:** builds a concise 10-line summary for the flight surgeon on Earth.
- **Offline-ready:** keeps working with no link to Earth and syncs when the next communication window opens. *It guides; it never diagnoses.*

### 🛰️ Strategic Planning & Hardware
Shows how the system operates on board: wireless biomedical sensors, power budget, and mission planning.

## 🧭 How to Use
1. Open the ASLS Hub (web browser, desktop or mobile).
2. Choose **Play the Game** to train through the interactive 3D missions, **Space Doc** to inspect the live health dashboard, or **Strategic Planning & Hardware** to review the onboard architecture.
